# Hartley summerhouse

Finished and handed over on 2026-11-09. Cedar shingles, which we said we'd never do again and then did.

## What went well

- Prefabricating the wall panels in the unit. Two days on site for the shell.

## What we'd change

- Drip edge on the flat roof is too shallow and the fascia is already staining. Look at this before the Okafor roof edge goes on.
- We under-priced the shingles by about a day of labour.
