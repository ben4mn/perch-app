# Pricing a garden room

A first number on the phone, before a drawing. It is always a range.

$$
P = A \times r + n_d \times d + E
$$

where $A$ is the floor area in square metres, $r$ the rate for the spec, $n_d$ the number of days on site, $d$ our day rate for two, and $E$ the electrics, which Dev prices on his own.

| Spec | Rate $r$ per m² | What it means |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Shell | £950 | Insulated, weathertight, no finishes inside |
| Standard | £1,350 | Lined, decorated, electric heating, LED lights |
| Fitted | £1,700 | Standard plus built-in desk, shelves and storage |

So a 4.8 x 3.6 m standard room, nine days on site:

$$
17.3 \times 1350 + 9 \times 640 + 2400 \approx 31{,}500
$$

About £31,500, before any surprises in the ground.

> [!tip] Charge for details
> Anything we haven't built before (a parapet, a green roof, a curved wall) gets its own line and at least a day. The Okafor parapet is the reason this note exists.
