# Handover checklist

Print two. One for the client's drawer, one for ours, both signed.

## Building

- [ ] Doors and windows open, close and lock, keys counted
- [ ] Roof outlet clear, gutter running
- [ ] No gaps in the cladding at the corners
- [ ] Floor level checked at four corners and the middle

## Electrics

- [ ] Certificate from Dev in the pack
- [ ] Every socket and light tested in front of the client
- [ ] Heater set to frost protection

## Paper

- [ ] Timber treatment and finish, and when to re-oil
- [ ] Guarantees for the glazing and the roof membrane
- [ ] Photos of the frame before it was closed in
- [ ] Our number, and what counts as an emergency

## Then

- [ ] Final invoice
- [ ] Ask if we can come back for photos
