# Loft insulation

Measured on a cold Saturday. There's about 100 mm of old wool between the joists, flattened in places.

- Loft floor is 6.1 m x 4.4 m, so about 27 m²
- Joists at 400 mm centres, 100 mm deep
- Plan: top up with 200 mm laid across the joists, keep a boarded walkway to the tank

Rolls are 1.14 m wide and 5 m long at 200 mm, so 5.7 m² each. 27 / 5.7 is 4.7, call it **6 rolls** with the eaves.

Don't block the vents at the eaves. Wear the mask.
