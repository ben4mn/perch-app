# Weeknight dal

Jo's version, which is better than mine.

- 250 g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, 3 cloves garlic, thumb of ginger
- 1 tsp each cumin seeds, turmeric, ground coriander
- Tin of tomatoes
- Lemon and a lot of coriander at the end

Lentils in 800 ml water with the turmeric, 20 minutes. Fry the onion slowly, then the spices, garlic and ginger, then the tomatoes. Stir it into the lentils. Lemon, salt, coriander.

Freezes well. Doubles without any trouble.
