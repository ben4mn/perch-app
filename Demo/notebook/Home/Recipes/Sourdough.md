# Sourdough

The weekday loaf. One loaf, about 900 g.

| Ingredient | Grams | Baker's % |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Strong white flour | 400 | 80% |
| Wholemeal | 100 | 20% |
| Water | 360 | 72% |
| Starter, fed the night before | 100 | 20% |
| Salt | 10 | 2% |

Hydration is water over flour: $h = \dfrac{w}{f} = \dfrac{360}{500} = 72\%$. Go to 75% in summer.

1. Mix flour and 340 g of the water, leave an hour
2. Add starter, salt and the rest of the water, squeeze it through
3. Four sets of folds, half an hour apart
4. Shape, basket, fridge overnight
5. 250 degrees in the pot, 20 minutes lid on, 25 off

If it spreads, the starter wasn't ready. Float a spoonful in water first.
