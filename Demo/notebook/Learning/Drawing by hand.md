# Drawing by hand

Goal: sketch a junction in front of a client, in pencil, in under two minutes, without apologising for it.

- Draw the section first. People understand cuts through a wall faster than 3D.
- Thick line for what's cut, thin for what's beyond
- Write the dimensions as you go, not at the end

Exercise from the book: one detail a day, eaves, verge, sill, threshold, roof edge, and then start again.
